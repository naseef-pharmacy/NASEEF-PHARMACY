About Us

Caring for Sharjah
Since 2009

What started as Naseef Pharmacy in Rolla, Al Nabba has grown into a family of three trusted pharmacies serving the Sharjah community. With Al Nabba Pharmacy and Al Siha W Al Afiya Pharmacy, we bring quality healthcare closer to you — no matter where you are in the city.

Our team of licensed pharmacists brings decades of combined experience, offering expert guidance in both English and Arabic. We partner with leading insurance providers and stock only MOH-approved medications from verified suppliers.

MOH Licensed & Approved

All three branches fully compliant with UAE Ministry of Health
